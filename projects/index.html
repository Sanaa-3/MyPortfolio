WEB APPLICATION

WinJar

I have been journaling for nine years, and I created WinJar based on my own experience with it. If you journal, you know that writing a full entry at the end of a long day can ironically feel like the biggest burden. WinJar removes that pressure by making it easier to record your wins and reflect on your progress.
